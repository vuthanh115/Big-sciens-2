Part 5: Present Progressive — am / is / are + verb-ing (5 points)

Part 6: Short Reading & Writing (3 points)
My Weekend: Hi! I am Leo. On Saturdays, my family and I go to the park. Right now, I am riding my bike and my sister is eating an apple. Tomorrow, we will visit our grandparents in the country!
  1. Where do Leo and his family go on Saturdays?
  2. What is Leo doing right now?
  3. Who will they visit tomorrow?
